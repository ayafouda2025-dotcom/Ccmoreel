# Kara Duo — brand notes for motion reels
Source: دليل الهوية البصرية (Edition 02 — 2026), 23 pages.

## Essence
- Saudi beauty brand, inspired by Saudi Khuzama (lavender, Horwoodia dicksoniae): four petals, calm color, lasting scent, blooms after rain.
- Promise: صُنع ليعتني بجمالك — Made to care for your beauty.
- Headline: الجمال عنايةٌ قبل أن يكون مظهراً.
- Personality: راقية، أنثوية، واثقة، قريبة. Values: الجودة أولاً، العناية في كل تفصيلة، الاعتزاز بالهوية السعودية.
- Audience: Saudi/Gulf woman who values quality, elegant look that reveals rather than hides.

## Logo
- "KARA" geometric wide-tracked caps (Montserrat-like) + "Duo" handwritten signature. One unit, never split, never retyped in another font.
- Approved versions: mauve on white (primary), Kara pink on white (marketing), white on mauve, pink on lavender (large only), black mono.
- Banned: rotate, stretch, two colors, shadow/glow, off-brand colors, on top of pattern, weak contrast, typed in another font.
- Clear space = height of K. Min 100px digital.
- Ads rule: logo must appear within the first 2 seconds of any video.

## Type (Google Fonts)
- Arabic display: Cairo Bold/SemiBold. Arabic text: Tajawal Regular/Medium. Arabic detail/prices: Almarai.
- Latin display: Playfair Display. Latin text: Montserrat (wide-tracked eyebrows). Signature: Allura (one word/short phrase).
- Max two faces per design (+ Latin signature). Arabic right-aligned; center only for short headlines. Arabic numerals (١٢٣) in designs, Latin in prices/contact.
- Scale: Display 52/68, H1 36/50, H2 24/36, Label 14/20, Body 17/30, Caption 13/22, Eyebrow 11, Signature 44.

## Graphic elements
- Khuzama flower: 4 lavender/pink petals with pink center (sticker, cover, seal).
- Sparkle star: 4-point pink star (max 2 per design).
- Arch frame: Saudi architectural arch, holds the product.
- Patterns: Khuzama field (light), dark version on mauve (luxury/gifts), stems pattern (thin curves + sparkles).

## Art direction
- One hero product centered in the arch; soft daylight/sunset light, no harsh shadows.
- Backgrounds in brand colors: lavender, ivory, light sand, silk fabric, khuzama sprigs.
- At least 1/3 breathing room for title + logo.
- Avoid: black/loud backgrounds, crowded frames, over-smoothed skin, stock photos.

## Tone of voice
Warm, clear, confident; feminine address, short sentences, no shouting, no "70% off" — offers framed as sets/gifts.
Say: "لمعة ناعمة تدوم معكِ طوال اليوم." / "مجموعة السهرة: ثلاث قطع تكمل إطلالتكِ." / "اكتشفي الدرجة التي تناسب بشرتكِ."
Signature lines: صُنع ليعتني بجمالك · Beauty Lives in Harmony / الجمال في انسجام · Inspired by Saudi Khuzama / من الخزامى السعودية · Timeless Beauty Together / جمال يدوم.
CTA: feminine verb, e.g. تسوّقي الآن، اكتشفي المجموعة، اطلبيه.
