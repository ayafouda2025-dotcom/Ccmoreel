Facts used (product page, 2026-11-14):

- main compartment with dividers you can rearrange
- four detachable zip pouches with clear fronts
- mesh and elastic pockets inside the lid for brushes and small accessories
- spinner wheels; telescopic pull handle + top carry handle
- divided interior keeps products from moving while travelling
- made for makeup artists: brides, events, shoots, working outside the studio

The creative idea: a retro game mission. She is a makeup artist with a bride at 6 pm and her kit is chaos.
The bag is her power-up: the dividers are a Tetris board that sorts the products, the pouches are an
inventory, the lid is a brush quiver, and the wheels turn the last scene into a side-scrolling run
(her real walk-cycle frames from the character sheet pull the bag) to "MISSION COMPLETE".
